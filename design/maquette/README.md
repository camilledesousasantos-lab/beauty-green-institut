# Design reference — the validated mock, extracted

Generated by `pnpm extract` (`scripts/extract-maquette.mjs`) from `public/maquette/desktop.html`
and `public/maquette/mobile.html`, the two Claude Design bundles Camille validated (21-22/09/2026).
Never edit these files by hand: re-run the script. The bundles themselves stay served at
`/maquette/desktop.html` and `/maquette/mobile.html`.

## The three facts that drive the build (checked by hand, 2026-09-30)

1. **Fonts** — Cormorant Garamond (the modules only use weights 500 and 600, normal and italic;
   the template also declares 400) + Archivo (300, 400, 500, 600, normal). Subsets `latin` and
   `latin-ext` carry French. Loaded through `next/font/google` in `app/layout.tsx`; the 13 woff2
   written to `fonts/` (gitignored) are the `next/font/local` fallback if the Google fetch fails
   at build (one `localFont()` per subset, `unicode-range` from `fonts.json`).
2. **Views** — 12 desktop views (router `router.jsx`: `index`, `prestations`, `electrolyse`,
   `soins-visage`, `cils`, `sourcils`, `blanchiment`, `institut`, `cheques-cadeaux`, `journal`,
   `article`, `formations`) + 2 mobile screens at 390 (`router-mobile.jsx`: Accueil, Électrolyse).
   Every other page's 390 composition is ours, derived from these two.
3. **Router props that `content.js` does not carry** (they live in `router.jsx` and go into the
   content files):
   - électrolyse — `image` institut-cabine, `proof` prestation-electrolyse-menton, `proofCaption`
     « Repousses sur le menton avant une séance. », `tarifsTitle` « Des séances à la durée »,
     `tarifsNote` « Le bilan du premier rendez-vous comprend la consultation et quinze minutes de
     séance offertes — 45 €. »
   - sourcils — `image` prestation-brow-lift, `tarifsTitle` « Prestations et combinaisons »,
     `tarifsNote` « Les prestations se combinent : la teinture seule ne comprend pas la restructuration. »
   - blanchiment — `image` prestation-blanchiment-dentaire-avant-apres, `tarifsTitle` « Séances et
     tarifs », `tarifsNote` « La deuxième séance n'est possible que dans les quatre semaines suivant la première. »
   - soins visage — `image` institut-cabine, `tarifsTitle` « Les trois soins »
   - cils — `image` prestation-rehaussement-cils, `tarifsTitle` « Rehaussement et teinture »

## Files

| File | What |
|------|------|
| `manifest.json` | every bundle entry: mime, size, detected role, sha256 prefix |
| `tokens.css` | the four `:root{…}` blocks of the template, verbatim (identical in both bundles) |
| `fonts.json` | families, weights, styles, subsets and the 37 `@font-face` rules (unicode ranges) |
| `content.js` | `window.BG` — every text of the 12 views (its 7 Journal articles are mock data: never shipped) |
| `modules/*.jsx` | the page modules as served, including the home patched on 21/09 (`home-desktop.jsx`) |
| `router.jsx`, `router-mobile.jsx` | the inline scripts that map views to modules and props |
| `ds/components/**` | the 13 design-system component files (15 exports), split from the DS bundle — gitignored |
| `fonts/*.woff2` | the 13 font files of the bundle — gitignored |

The DS bundle also embeds older copies of the `ui_kits/site/*` sources: they predate the 21/09
patch of the home and are not extracted — `modules/` wins.
